GitHub shipped a new Copilot impact dashboard this week (July 22) for enterprise admins, and the framing is a little spicy. Instead of one big "active users" number, it sorts your team into adoption phases — code-first, agent-first, multi-agent — plus a "passive" bucket for people who hold a license but basically never touch the thing.

The honest read: a single "active" number was hiding a lot, and now managers can see exactly who's actually getting value versus who's just paying for a seat. Great for justifying spend, slightly ominous if you're the person quietly ignoring your Copilot license.

If your company runs Copilot at scale, expect this dashboard to show up in a meeting near you soon.
