Anthropic quietly kept Claude Sonnet 5's launch pricing alive this whole time — $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output — and it's set to expire August 31. After that it jumps to the standard $3/$15.

What's wild is Sonnet 5 is already the default model in Claude Code, ships with a native 1M-token context window, and is landing close to Opus 4.8 on coding benchmarks for a fraction of the price. If you've been telling yourself you'll get around to trying it, this month is the cheapest that's ever going to happen.

I'm half expecting a scramble in the last week of August once people realize the clock's actually running out.
