OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna pricing by 80% this week and moved Codex's Auto-review feature onto it — the automated pass that checks your agent's work before you do. Combined, that's roughly a 10x cost drop for anyone leaning on Auto-review to keep Codex's agentic runs in check.

Timing feels pointed too: this lands right as Claude Sonnet 5's own discount window is closing at the end of August. The AI coding tools aren't just racing on capability anymore, they're racing on who can make babysitting an agent cheapest.
