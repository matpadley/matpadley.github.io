GitHub Copilot has added Claude Haiku 5.5, the small and speedy member of the Claude family. It's pitched at the everyday stuff: quick edits, terminal jobs, and little helper agents that do the grunt work. GitHub says that in early testing it matched Claude Sonnet 5 on lots of coding tasks while using far fewer tokens and steps.

It's showing up for Pro, Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise, in pretty much every place Copilot lives (VS Code, JetBrains, Xcode, the CLI, even the mobile app). Rollout is gradual, so if you can't see it in the model picker yet, give it a day or two.

Quick tip from me: for the boring jobs, try the small model first. Your wallet might thank you.
