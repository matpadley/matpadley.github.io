If you sign into Codex with a ChatGPT account, today's the day GPT-5.4 and GPT-5.4-mini officially retire — OpenAI wants everyone moved over to GPT-5.6 Terra and Luna instead. API-key users are unaffected, but anyone with agents, configs, or scheduled tasks pinned to the old models needs to update them now or things could quietly break. Out with the old, in with the (apparently) better.
