OpenAI took the handcuffs off Codex and ChatGPT Work this week — the annoying rolling 5-hour usage limit is gone for Plus, Pro, and Business, and they even did a mid-announcement usage reset so everyone started fresh. On paper, great news if you like marathon coding sessions.

The catch, and there's always a catch: it's explicitly *temporary*, the weekly cap is now the real ceiling, and everything still draws from one shared credit pool. So when the new desktop ChatGPT Voice fires off a voice-triggered agent, that's eating the same budget as your actual coding. Freedom, with an asterisk.

Worth knowing before you plan your week around "unlimited" Codex — you're really just trading one limit for another.
